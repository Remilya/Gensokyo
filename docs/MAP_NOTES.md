## 2. Reconciling Disagreements Across Fan Maps & Canon (`PMiSS` / *Strange Creators of Outer World*)

Different Touhou maps rotate the compass or swap local neighborhoods because Gensokyo has no single official ZUN grid map, though *Perfect Memento in Strict Sense* (`PMiSS`), *Wild and Horned Hermit*, *Forbidden Scrollery*, and *Lotus Eaters* establish clear relational geography:

1. **Hakurei Shrine (`博麗神社`)**:
   - *Canon (`PMiSS` / `FS`)*: Located on the **eastern border mountain ridge** of Gensokyo, sitting directly on the Great Hakurei Barrier between Gensokyo and the Outside World, with a long stone staircase descending west toward the Human Village via the Shrine Approach and Forest of Magic edge.
   - *Reconciliation*: Placed in the **East / East-Northeast Border Ridge** (`x: 0.84, y: 0.44`), overlooking the basin with the Pond Behind the Shrine (`Genji's Pond`), Three Fairies' Oak Tree, and Moriya Branch Shrine nearby.
2. **Youkai Mountain (`妖怪の山`)**:
   - *Canon*: Towers over the **Northern / Northwestern** skyline of Gensokyo. Its summit crater holds **Wind God's Lake** (`x: 0.36, y: 0.22`) and **Moriya Shrine** (`x: 0.33, y: 0.24`), fed by the **Waterfall of Nine Heavens** (`x: 0.38, y: 0.29`), with **Genbu Ravine** (`x: 0.44, y: 0.35`) and the **Underground Geyser Center** (`x: 0.41, y: 0.31`) at its mid-slopes/base.
3. **Human Village (`人間の里`)**:
   - *Canon*: Sits in the fertile **Central Basin** (`x: 0.50, y: 0.54`), protected by walls/gates and bisected by the Dragon Canal (`Canal of Willows`). Surrounding or inside it are **Myouren Temple** (`x: 0.44, y: 0.60`, with the Cemetery and Hall of Dreams' Great Mausoleum beneath), **Hieda Mansion**, **Suzunaan**, **Geidontei**, **Dragon Statue Square**, and the **Moriya Ropeway Village Station** (`x: 0.43, y: 0.48`).
4. **Kourindou & Forest of Magic (`香霖堂` / `魔法の森`)**:
   - *Canon*: **Kourindou** (`x: 0.57, y: 0.50`) stands expressly at the border between the Human Village and the **Forest of Magic** (`x: 0.62, y: 0.47`), which holds **Marisa's House** (`x: 0.60, y: 0.46`), **Alice's House / Margatroid Residence** (`x: 0.64, y: 0.43`), and **Cemetery / Mushroom Clearings**.
5. **Misty Lake & Scarlet Devil Mansion (`霧の湖` / `紅魔館`)**:
   - *Canon (`PMiSS`)*: **Misty Lake** (`x: 0.49, y: 0.39`) lies at the foot of Youkai Mountain between the mountain slopes and the central forests; the **Scarlet Devil Mansion** (`x: 0.46, y: 0.36`) sits on its shoreline/ridge, with the **Prismriver Mansion** (`x: 0.53, y: 0.38`) and **Spring Path** (`x: 0.45, y: 0.42`) nearby.
6. **Bamboo Forest of the Lost & Eientei (`迷いの竹林` / `永遠亭`)**:
   - *Canon*: Lies in the **South / Southeast Basin** (`x: 0.58, y: 0.70`) opposite Youkai Mountain across the Human Village, concealing **Eientei** (`x: 0.63, y: 0.73`), **Mokou's Hut** (`x: 0.56, y: 0.67`), and adjacent to the **Garden of the Sun** (`x: 0.50, y: 0.75`) and **Nameless Hill** (`x: 0.44, y: 0.78`).
7. **Road of Liminality, Muenzuka, Sanzu River & Higan (`無縁塚` / `三途の川` / `彼岸`)**:
   - *Canon*: Lies at the far edge of the barrier (`Northwest / North-Northwest` beyond the Road of Reconsideration and Untrodden Valley), where the **Sanzu River** (`x: 0.16, y: 0.22`) separates living Gensokyo (`Muenzuka`, `x: 0.24, y: 0.18`) from **Higan** (`x: 0.09, y: 0.18`), **Netherworld / Hakugyokurou** (`x: 0.20, y: 0.09`), and the **Animal Realm** (`x: 0.11, y: 0.08`).
8. **Underground / Former Hell (`旧地獄` / `地霊殿`) & Otherworlds (`魔界` / `月の都` / `天界`)**:
   - *Design Solution*: Following classic Touhou cartographic atlases, we place the **Underground**, **Makai**, **Lunar Capital**, and **Heaven** in dedicated, illustrated **Realm Cartouche Zones** along the outer atlas margins (connected via clickable portals like the *Underground Geyser Center / Fantastic Blowhole*, *Palace of the Dragon's Road / Heaven*, *Lunar Passage*, and *Portal to Makai*), while also keeping every location on the unified `2400 × 1600` coordinate canvas (`0..1`) so pan-and-zoom, search, and overland route planning work seamlessly across all **116 locations**.

## 3. Canonical Canvas & Anchor Specification

- **Logical SVG Canvas**: `2400 × 1600` (`3:2` aspect ratio)
- **Normalized Coordinates**: `x ∈ [0, 1]`, `y ∈ [0, 1]`
- **Scale Calibration**: `0.10` canvas units ≈ `1.2 km` (~15–18 minutes walking on maintained roads, ~25–30 minutes on mountain/forest trails), calibrated to *Perfect Memento in Strict Sense* and official print-work travel descriptions.
